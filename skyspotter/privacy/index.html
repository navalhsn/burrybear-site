Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Octablend ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use SkySpotter (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide the App's core functionality:

DataPurpose
CameraUsed in real time to identify aircraft in view. Camera images are processed to detect and identify nearby aircraft and are not stored or uploaded by us unless you explicitly save or share a result.
Precise locationUsed to determine which aircraft are near you, so the App can show accurate flight data for your surroundings. Location is used while the App is in use and is not shared with third parties for advertising.
Subscription and purchase dataApple and our subscription platform (RevenueCat) process your subscription status, purchase history, and entitlement information to unlock SkySpotter Pro features. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
Usage and diagnostic dataBasic app analytics and crash/performance data (for example, via Firebase) help us understand how the App is used and fix issues. This data is generally aggregated or pseudonymous.
Device identifiersUsed to associate your subscription entitlement with your device/account and to keep the App functioning correctly across sessions.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's core aircraft-identification and flight-tracking features.
  • To process and manage your SkySpotter Pro subscription.
  • To monitor and improve the App's performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following categories of third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Flight data providers — supply live aircraft tracking information used to identify planes near you.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status and entitlements on our behalf.
  • Apple (App Store) — processes all subscription payments; we never see or store your full payment details.
  • Firebase (Google) — may be used for basic analytics and crash reporting to help us improve the App.

4. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your camera images, precise location history, or personal data with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain the minimum data necessary to operate the App and manage your subscription. Camera frames used for on-device or in-session aircraft identification are not retained by us beyond the identification process unless you choose to save or share a result within the App.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can disable camera or location access at any time in your device's Settings, though this will limit or disable core App functionality.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID account settings.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
